# Autoimmunity Mechanisms: Viral-Driven MS Pathogenesis

## Overview

Mechanistic pathways linking viral infection to loss of self-tolerance and myelin-directed autoimmunity.

## Molecular Mimicry

### Sequence Homology Between Viral and Myelin Antigens
- **HSV-1 and Myelin Basic Protein (MBP)**
  - Identified homologous epitopes
  - Cross-reactive T cell recognition
  - Structural similarity analysis
  - Predicted functional epitopes

- **Other Viral-Myelin Homologies**
  - HHV-6 proteins and MOG
  - CMV glycoproteins and MBP
  - Myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein (MOG) cross-reactivity
  - Protein domains and functional units

### Structural Basis of Cross-Reactivity
- TCR recognition of shared epitopes
- MHC-peptide binding predictions
- Crystal structures of cross-reactive complexes
- Experimental TCR-peptide-MHC studies

### Validation Approaches
- Synthetic peptide stimulation of patient lymphocytes
- Recombinant viral protein recognition
- Adoptive transfer experiments in animal models
- Direct TCR sequencing (single-cell, bulk)

## Bystander Activation

### Innate Immune Priming
- PAMPs and DAMPs from viral infection
- PRR-mediated dendritic cell maturation
- TLR signaling and inflammatory environment
- Adjuvant-like effects of viremia

### Antigen-Presenting Cell Activation
- Upregulation of costimulatory molecules
- Increased MHC expression
- Production of inflammatory cytokines
- Enhanced antigen processing

### Nonspecific T Cell Activation
- CD4+ and CD8+ T cell expansion
- Myelin-reactive T cells in activated state
- High-affinity TCR engagement
- Reduced TCR threshold for activation

### CNS Inflammatory Environment
- Chemokine gradients directing infiltration
- Activated immune cells in lesions
- Local antigen presentation to myelin-reactive T cells
- Perpetuation of autoimmunity

## Epitope Spreading

### Intramolecular Spreading
- Initial response to dominant viral epitope
- Recruitment of subdominant determinants
- Sequential wave of immune recognition
- Driving factor: persistent antigen and inflammation

### Intermolecular Spreading
- From viral to cross-reactive myelin epitope
- From one myelin protein to another
- From native to cryptic determinants
- Expansion of autoimmune repertoire

### Role in MS Progression
- Contribution to relapsing-remitting course
- Amplification of CNS inflammation
- Perpetuation despite viral clearance
- Therapeutic implications for early intervention

## Loss of Immune Tolerance

### Central Tolerance Defects
- Thymic selection of autoreactive T cells
- AIRE (Autoimmune Regulator) dysfunction
- Medullary thymic epithelial cells (mTECs)
- Escape of myelin-reactive cells to periphery

### Peripheral Tolerance Mechanisms
- Regulatory T cell (Treg) function
  - CD4+ CD25+ Foxp3+ populations
  - IL-10 and TGF-β production
  - Suppressive capacity in MS
  - Viral infection effects on Tregs

- Immune Regulatory Checkpoints
  - CTLA-4 and PD-1 signaling
  - Immunoregulatory cytokines
  - Tolerogenic dendritic cells
  - Failure in MS pathogenesis

### Viral-Induced Loss of Tolerance
- Transient Treg depletion during acute infection
- Prolonged Treg dysfunction in chronic infection
- Bystander T cell expansion overcoming tolerance
- Creation of autoreactive memory cells

## Viral-Specific T Cell Responses

### CD8+ T Cell Responses
- Antigen-specific CTL expansion
- Perforin and granzyme cytotoxicity
- Contribution to oligodendrocyte killing
- Persistent in chronic viral infection

### CD4+ T Cell Responses
- Th1 (IFN-γ, TNF-α)
- Th17 (IL-17, IL-22)
- Th2 (IL-4, IL-10)
- Polarization in viral infection
- MS-associated Th1/Th17 skewing

### T Cell Exhaustion
- PD-1, TIM-3, LAG-3 expression
- Reduced effector function
- Gene expression signatures
- Reversal by checkpoint blockade

## Autoimmune B Cell Responses

### Anti-Myelin Antibodies
- Anti-MOG antibodies
- Anti-MBP antibodies
- Anti-aquaporin-4 (in NMO spectrum)
- Intrathecal production

### B Cell Activation in Viral Infection
- TLR signaling (TLR7, TLR9)
- T helper cell cooperation
- Bystander activation
- Production of polyreactive antibodies

### Antibody-Mediated Demyelination
- Complement activation (classical pathway)
- ADCC (antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity)
- Direct opsonization of myelin
- Oligodendrocyte lysis

## Innate-Adaptive Immune Crosstalk

### Type I Interferon Signaling
- IFN-α/β production in viral infection
- IFNAR-mediated signaling
- Enhanced adaptive immune responses
- Th17 differentiation promotion
- Treg suppression effects

### Inflammasome Activation
- NLRP3 inflammasome
- IL-1β and IL-18 production
- Th17 polarization
- Chronic CNS inflammation

### Trained Immunity
- Innate immune memory
- Enhanced responsiveness to secondary stimuli
- Epigenetic modifications
- Relevance to MS flares
